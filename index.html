About

Full-Stack Developer.
Building practical web systems.

I’m a Full-Stack Web Developer focused on building real websites and simple web systems that actually solve business problems. I work mainly with PHP, MySQL, JavaScript, and WordPress — building everything from business websites to custom tools for clients. More recently I’ve been rounding that out with Supabase, Redis, Railway, and Render for backend data, caching, and deployment.

Over the years, I’ve worked on client projects ranging from small business websites to more structured systems like a digital restaurant menu platform. Most of my experience comes from freelance work, where I handle everything from planning and development to deployment and maintenance.

I started out working in a more structured environment where I supported backend development and also helped teach web development basics. That early experience shaped how I approach building systems — keeping things simple, functional, and easy to maintain.

Based in Enugu, Nigeria. I mostly work remotely and collaborate with clients on building and maintaining web projects.
